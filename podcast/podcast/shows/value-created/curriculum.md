# Value, Created (working title)

**Status (2026-09-26):** Curriculum approved by Long. Production DEFERRED until the show-making process is solid (post Money, Mastered). Do not start production before Long greenlights it.

**Premise:** *Money, Mastered* taught how money works; this teaches what money is made of.

**Format (planned):** Same playbook as Money, Mastered — hosts Alex + Jordan (avocado_v2:MAI_01 / MAI_03), ~15 min per chapter, -16 LUFS / 128 kbps, self-hosted feed on thejoydigi.com. Cadence TBD (1–2/week or after Money, Mastered wraps mid-Dec 2026).

## Part I — Seeing Value
1. What "Value" Actually Means — value isn't in things, it's in people; the diamond-water paradox.
2. The Beholder's Eye — marginal utility: why the tenth slice of pizza is worth less than the first.
3. Two Faces: Use vs. Exchange — a warm coat vs. a stock certificate; confusing them costs you.
4. Price Is Not Value — price is a rumor about value; mispricings are where opportunity lives.
5. Needs, Wants, and Pain — the three wells value springs from, and why pain is the deepest.

## Part II — Creating Value
6. Every Value Starts as a Problem Solved — the one-sentence theory of the whole book.
7. The Inputs: Time, Effort, Skill — what you actually spend, and why skill is the only input that compounds.
8. Knowledge as Raw Material — the most valuable input weighs nothing.
9. Making Things People Want — product thinking: building the right thing, not just building.
10. Service, by Hand — why doing things for people never fully automates away.
11. Attention Is an Asset — infinite content, finite attention; scarcity is value.
12. Trust, the Invisible Ingredient — the most valuable line item that never appears on a balance sheet.
13. Removing Friction — every wait, click, and hassle you erase is value created.
14. Recombination — innovation is mostly rearrangement; learn to combine.

## Part III — Capturing Value
15. Creating Isn't Enough — why the people who make things go broke: the capture problem.
16. Pricing: Naming Your Worth — cost-plus vs. value-based; charging what it's worth.
17. Scarcity and Moats — what stops competitors from eating what you built.
18. Systems That Run Without You — turning effort into process; the step from job to asset.
19. Brand: Trust, Stored — what a brand really is and how it compounds.
20. Ownership — equity and assets; why owners keep what workers create.

## Part IV — Exchanging and Scaling
21. Trade: The First Leverage — do one thing well, trade for the rest.
22. Money: Value, Frozen — how money stores created value across time. (Handshake chapter with Money, Mastered.)
23. Markets — where value finds its price.
24. Leverage — capital, code, people, media: doing more with less.
25. Distribution — value nobody sees might as well not exist.
26. Networks — products that get more valuable the more people use them.
27. Compounding — time as the final multiplier.

## Part V — Mastery
28. Give Value First — generosity as strategy; why givers end up with the most. (Long's Nextdoor play, codified.)
29. The Reputation Flywheel — delivered value becomes reputation becomes opportunity.
30. Choosing What to Build — taste, judgment, and picking problems worth your life.
31. Your Value-Creation System — assembling the pieces into one personal machine.
32. Teach It — the final leverage; why teaching multiplies value. The reason this book exists.
