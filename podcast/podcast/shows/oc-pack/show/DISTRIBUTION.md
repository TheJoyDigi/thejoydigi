# The OC Pack — Distribution Strategy

Written 2026-09-25 from industry research (index-sourced; re-verify platform
rules before acting — they change). Long's standing rules: value-first, never
spammy or scammy, help people who need it. No active promotion until the show
has **5 episodes** at full quality.

## The 5-episode rule (validated)

Industry consensus backs Long's call: launch the promo push with 3–5 episodes
live (binge-ability, social proof, production buffer). Keep the weekly cadence —
consistent weekly drops compound better than dumping everything at once. The
5-episode back catalog is the asset; the push is the announcement.

## Channel playbook (ranked for a hyperlocal pet show)

### 1. Local partnerships (highest impact)
- **Rescues/shelters**: offer an "adoptable dog of the week" mention; they share
  the episode with their audience. Start with Pet Adoption Center of Orange County
  (already a source relationship from Ep 1).
- **Vet offices, pet stores, groomers, trainers**: counter QR cards linking to the
  show (Apple Podcasts Connect generates QR codes/badges free).
- Pitch: we send pet parents to them; they put a card on the counter. No money.

### 2. Short video clips (Instagram Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
- Talking-head style clips, 15–60s, hook in the first 2 seconds, full insight
  inside the clip (never withhold the payoff), big readable captions, local
  hashtags + geo-tags.
- **Record video from the start** — audiograms are practically invisible in these
  feeds. (Production note: add a simple video capture step to the pipeline before
  Phase 1.)
- 3 clips per episode is the sustainable cadence (~1 hr/week with AI help).

### 3. Nextdoor
- Claim a free **Business Page** for the show; Business Posts max ~1x/week framed
  as community announcements (e.g. "this week's local pet events").
- **Never self-promote from the personal account** — that's an explicit reportable
  reason on Nextdoor.
- Use the Events calendar for pet events we cover. Note: Nextdoor's live-animal
  business ban exempts pet shelters/stores.

### 4. Apple Podcasts / Spotify listing SEO
- Title is already keyword-forward ("The OC Pack: Orange County Dog & Pet Parents") — no rename.
- Apple category: Leisure → Pets & Animals (specific beats general).
- Topic words in the first 2 sentences of the description; substance-led episode titles.
- Add a 30–90s **trailer** episode (RSS trailer type).
- Pitch Apple's free editorial feature form ≥ 2 weeks before the push. Featuring is
  free editorial — never pay a "get featured" service.

### 5. Facebook groups (OC pet parent groups)
- Answer-first: help people, start conversations, no "shielded promotions"
  (disguised ads), no poaching members. Links only where genuinely useful.

### 6. Cross-promo with similar-size shows
- Ladder: trailer swaps → guest spots → feed drops. Pitch with 2–3 specific ideas,
  not a generic "let's collaborate."

### 7. Reddit (long game only)
- 9:1 value-to-promo ratio, always disclose affiliation, never post the same link
  twice in 24h (shadowban), never fake upvotes (permaban).
- Check r/orangecounty's sidebar rules before any promo post (could not be verified
  in research — do this live before acting).

## Content engine (per episode, ~1 hr/week)

One 20-min episode becomes:
- 3 talking clips (Reels/TikTok/Shorts)
- 1 quote card
- 1 native-text "local finds" post (Nextdoor/FB — no bare links)
- 1 show-notes post with local keywords (blog/SEO)
- 1 story poll

Free tools (re-verify tiers before use): Opus Clip (free tier, watermarked),
Headliner (free-forever plan), Captions (free tier, limited exports).
**Always review AI-made clips before posting.**

## Metrics that matter (5)

1. Follows/subscriber growth rate
2. Completion rate (target ≥60–80%; industry average ~72%)
3. 7-day downloads per episode
4. Apple ratings/reviews velocity (last 60 days weighted)
5. One owned conversion: email list signups or QR scans

Benchmarks (Buzzsprout global stats, Aug 2026): median 27 / top 25% 96 / top 10%
407 first-7-day downloads. For a niche local show, 25–100/episode by month 6 is
genuinely good. Expect a 4–6 week lag before clipping moves download numbers;
real growth compounds over 1–2 years.

## Anti-patterns (instant distrust or bans)

- Buying fake reviews (Apple uses human + software fraud detection; bans repeat offenders)
- Click-farm downloads for chart gaming
- Reddit same-link spam / sockpuppet upvotes
- Nextdoor personal-account self-promo
- Facebook disguised promos / member poaching
- Engagement pods, keyword-stuffed titles (Apple has removed shows for this)
- Teaser clips that withhold the payoff
- Paid "get featured on Apple" services

## 90-day plan

### Phase 0 — Episodes 1–5 (now; NO active promotion)
- Lock quality: −16 LUFS, stingers, hook in 60–90s, fact-check gates.
- Record video alongside audio (for future clips).
- Publish a 30–90s trailer; set Apple category to Leisure → Pets & Animals.
- Test clipping tools on Ep 1–2 (clips banked, not posted).
- Seed Reddit/FB/Nextdoor presence with helpful answers, zero links.
- Line up 10 partnership targets (rescues, vets, stores, trainers).
- Start an email list (even a simple signup).
- Bank 1–2 buffer episodes.

### Phase 1 — Launch push (at 5 episodes)
- Announcement burst: trailer + "5 episodes live" posts on owned channels.
- 3–5 clips/week begin.
- QR counter cards → vet offices / pet stores; rescue adoptable-dog segment asks.
- Cross-promo pitches (trailer swaps first).
- Apple editorial pitch (≥2 weeks out).
- Add a ratings ask to the outro.
- Local PR: OC pet bloggers, community newsletters.

### Phase 2 — Sustain (ongoing)
- Weekly episode + repurposing routine (the content engine above).
- Monthly metrics review against the 5 metrics.
- Re-pitch Apple editorial after 6–8 weeks if no feature.
- Keep partnerships warm: fresh QR cards, new rescue dogs, event tie-ins.
