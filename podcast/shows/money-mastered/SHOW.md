# Money, Mastered — show stub

36-chapter book teaching macroeconomics, money, investing, trading, and BTC from zero
to mastery, as a podcast.

- **Hosts:** Alex (`avocado_v2:MAI_01`) + Jordan (`avocado_v2:MAI_03`)
- **Cadence:** 3 chapters/week — Mon / Wed / Fri, ~8:45 AM America/Los_Angeles
- **Length:** ~15 min per chapter
- **Audio standard:** -16 LUFS / 128 kbps (every chapter, no exceptions)
- **Served output:** `public/podcasts/money-mastered/` (MP3s + `feed.xml`)
- **Chapters follow the book curriculum strictly.** Teasers never promise chapters out
  of order. If a teaser and the curriculum disagree, the curriculum wins.

## Production

Follows the studio pipeline (`podcast/README.md`) and the crew playbooks
(`podcast/agents/`). Chapter scripts, claim ledgers, and production notes live here
under `chapters/` as they are produced; finished audio and the feed live in
`public/podcasts/money-mastered/`.

Spotify: the show has been subject to false content-protection flags (title similarity
with an unrelated book). Chapters are appealed when flagged; the show name stays unless
Long decides otherwise.
