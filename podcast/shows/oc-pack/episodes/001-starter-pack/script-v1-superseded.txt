Jordan: Now the safety minute, because this one is Orange County specific. Foxtails.
Alex: Foxtails are barbed grass seeds, and California is basically ground zero for them. They peak from May into early summer, but the dried ones stay dangerous through the fall. The barbs only move in one direction, so they burrow into ears, noses, paws, and skin, and they do not come out on their own.
Jordan: What should people watch for?
Alex: Sudden head shaking or pawing at an ear, violent sneezing or a bloody nose, squinting or eye discharge, or sudden paw licking and limping. A simple removal runs one hundred to three hundred dollars, but the average insurance claim is around four hundred seventy dollars, and severe cases have topped seventeen thousand dollars. So after every walk near dry grass, check the paws, ears, nose, and belly. Thirty seconds that can save you thousands.
