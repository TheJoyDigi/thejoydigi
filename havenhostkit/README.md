# HavenHostKit

HavenHostKit is a digital product line for Airbnb and short-term rental hosts: a printable welcome book, house signs, and a turnover checklist that hosts personalize with their own property details (WiFi, house rules, local recommendations) and print for their guests. It is a product of **Operation Ten-A-Day** and is sold through the Etsy shop **HavenHostKit** (etsy.com/shop/HavenHostKit).

This folder holds the v2 product package, pushed November 21, 2026, so the owner can download and print the files to judge the look and usefulness firsthand before the listings go live.
