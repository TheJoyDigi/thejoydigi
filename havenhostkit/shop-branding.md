## About / our story (paste into the About section)

HavenHostKit is a new digital shop, opened in November 2026, and we would rather tell you that than pretend to have a long history.

We make printable templates for Airbnb and VRBO hosts: welcome books that answer guest questions before they are asked, friendly signs that explain the house without awkward conversations, and turnover checklists that help a clean stay happen the same way every time.

The idea is simple. Most hosts did not get into hosting to spend a weekend writing a guest guide, so we wrote it for you. Every product is a finished PDF with clear placeholders where your details go: your WiFi, your check-out time, your favorite coffee place. Fill them in, print, and you are done.

We read every message ourselves. If a file is not right, tell us and we will make it right.

## Shop FAQ (paste into the shop FAQ / policies area)

**Q: Are your products physical items?**
A: No. Everything in this shop is a digital PDF download. Nothing is printed or shipped. You download instantly after purchase and print what you need.

**Q: Are the templates editable in Canva?**
A: No, and we say that plainly on every listing. Our PDFs are finished and print-ready, not design files. You personalize them by filling in the [bracket placeholders] with a free PDF reader, or by printing and writing your details in by hand.

**Q: What do I need to use these files?**
A: A free PDF reader to view and fill in placeholders, and a printer or a local print shop for paper copies. All pages are US Letter size.

**Q: Can I use one purchase at more than one property?**
A: Yes, at properties you own or manage. Please do not resell, redistribute, or share the files themselves.

**Q: What if a page does not apply to my property?**
A: Do not print it. Every book and pack is built so pages and signs can be used on their own.

**Q: What is your refund policy?**
A: Because these are digital downloads, we cannot offer refunds. But if anything is wrong with your files, message us and we will fix it or replace it. We would rather make it right than lose a host.

**Q: How fast do you reply to messages?**
A: We read and answer shop messages ourselves, normally within one business day.

**Q: Do you take custom requests?**
A: Not yet. We are a new shop building our core range first. Tell us what you wish existed, though: our next products come from host requests.

## Logo spec (to be produced next, no stock imagery)

- **Form:** a simple wordmark. "HavenHostKit" set in a warm serif (Fraunces if licensed and available, otherwise a clean Times-style serif as used in the current product PDFs), ink #1A2E28, on warm cream #FAF6F0.
- **Mark:** above or left of the wordmark, one small line icon drawn in terracotta #C67B5C at a light stroke: a simple house outline with a heart or a keyhole inside. One icon only, no clip art, no stock imagery, no photograph.
- **Accent:** a short muted gold #C9A86A rule under the wordmark, matching the rule used on the product pages.
- **Versions to produce:** (1) square shop icon version, mark only, centered on cream, at Etsy's requested shop icon size; (2) horizontal wordmark version for documents and the banner. Both exported as PNG with the cream background filled, not transparent, so Etsy does not render it on white.
- **Seller photo slot:** use the square logo mark version. Do not use a personal photo of the owner.
- **Do not:** no gradient, no drop shadow, no script font, no Airbnb or VRBO logo or anything that implies a platform endorsement.

## Banner spec (to be produced next, no stock imagery)

- **Canvas:** Etsy's big banner size, 3360 x 840 px, with all text kept inside the center safe area.
- **Background:** warm cream #FAF6F0, flat. Optional: a very light sage #8A9B8E tint band along the bottom edge, no more than 10 percent of the height.
- **Left or center:** the HavenHostKit wordmark in ink, with the gold rule, same as the logo.
- **Tagline under the wordmark, in ink, plain words:** "Printable welcome books, signs, and checklists for Airbnb and VRBO hosts."
- **Right side:** simple flat mock shapes of the three products (a book cover rectangle, a framed sign rectangle, a checklist page with terracotta checkboxes), drawn from our own product pages in the design colors. These are drawings of our own files, not stock photos and not photographs of anyone's property.
- **Do not:** no stock photography, no fake review stars, no "bestseller" or sales claims, no discount banners until a real sale event exists.
