**Jordan:** Put the fixed supply number on the table, because it is the number most people have heard.

**Jordan:** So scarcity is not just a number typed somewhere. It is a number thousands of independent computers enforce, and breaking it means leaving the network.

**Jordan:** That makes digital scarcity click. Gold is scarce because nature made only so much of it and digging up more is slow and expensive. Bitcoin is scarce because the rules make more impossible beyond the cap, and checking the supply is cheap and open to anyone.
