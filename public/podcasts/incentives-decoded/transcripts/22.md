**Alex:** So here is the move. Three principles for designing incentives that work.

**Jordan:** One. Reward outcomes, not activity. Do not pay for hours. Pay for results. Do not praise being busy. Praise finishing. When people own the outcome, they find the fastest path. When they are paid for activity, they find the longest one.
