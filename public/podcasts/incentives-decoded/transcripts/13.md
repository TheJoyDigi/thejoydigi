**Jordan:** Here is why this always happens. A metric is a proxy. It stands in for something you actually care about but cannot measure directly.

**Alex:** Test scores stand in for learning. Units per hour stand in for good work. New accounts stand in for customer trust. The proxy is always simpler than the real thing. And simpler means gameable.

**Jordan:** The moment you reward the proxy, smart people stop pursuing the goal and start pursuing the proxy. Not because they are dishonest. Because you told them to. You said this number is what matters. They believed you.

**Alex:** And the cruel twist? The better the metric, the faster it dies. A vague metric is hard to game. A precise metric is a precise instruction for how to cheat without technically cheating.
