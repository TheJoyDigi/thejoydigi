**Jordan:** Here is the sentence this chapter hangs on. A brand is not what you say about yourself. It is what people believe about you, and belief is trust with a memory. **Alex:** So what is a brand actually made of. Three parts, and they have to stack in order. The first is the promise. What you claim, explicitly or not, every time someone buys from you. **Alex:** Now watch what happens when this runs for ten years. Two plumbers start in the same town. Ray runs ads.
