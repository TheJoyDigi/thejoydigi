**Alex:** Last chapter we said the best fit wins, and fit is discovered by watching people. But there is a kind of value that cannot be discovered, packaged, or shipped. **Jordan:** We live in the age of automation, and the pitch is always the same. Faster, cheaper, no humans required. Self checkout. Chatbots. Automated everything. **Alex:** Why? Because service is not a transaction. It is a relationship, compressed into a moment. When the Nordstrom clerk refunded those tires, the value was not the seventy five dollars. **Alex:** And here is the economic secret. The extra cannot be automated away, because automation is the enemy of the extra. A system does exactly what it is programmed to do, no more. **Jordan:** This is why doing things for people never fully automates away. Not because humans are better at the task. The machine is often faster and more accurate.
