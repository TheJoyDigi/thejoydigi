# Content Strategy — Worth It, Season 1

*Director: Lola. The content brain of the show.*

## The job of the season
Move a 20-something from "money feels like something that happens to other
people" to "I can see value, create it, and price it" — in twelve 30-second
episodes, each one a complete myth → reframe → flip → move.

## The arc: see → create → capture → give
| Block | Eps | Job | Episodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEE | 1–3 | The lens: where value actually lives | 1 · coffee — problem solved = value; 2 · diamond — value lives in people; 3 · sale price — price ≠ value |
| CREATE | 4–7 | The work: making things worth paying for | 4 · slow rich; 5 · boring rich — erase friction; 6 · trust, the invisible dividend; 7 · originality |
| CAPTURE | 8–11 | The price: naming what it's worth | 8 · a raise is a price; 9 · great work doesn't pay; 10 · salary ≠ prize; 11 · the most expensive choice |
| GIVE | 12 | The move that compounds | 12 · give it away — both leads, one frame |

Each block hands the viewer a new question: SEE → "who is it worth it to?";
CREATE → "what friction can I erase?"; CAPTURE → "what's my price?";
GIVE → "what can I give first?"

## Wren & Kai — the two leads
- Odd episodes: **Wren** (narrator Leda). Even episodes: **Kai** (narrator Puck).
- Two peers learning the same lens — never gurus lecturing. The rotation is
  the strategy: no single voice, the show feels like a conversation across
  episodes, and the audience gets to pick a favorite.
- Light personalities, thesis first. Wren notices and questions; Kai builds
  and prices. Neither carries an episode — the idea does.
- **Finale (director's call, 2026-10-02): Wren and Kai share a frame in
  Ep 12.** Two people who learned to see value, meeting on the other side.
  The give-first move, embodied together.

## The 85-word rule
~32 s cut, ~28.5 s narration → **~85 words maximum**. Every word earns its
place: myth, reframe, flip, move, refrain. Cut connective tissue first
("that's the game", "that's how money moves"); never cut the refrain
("Problem solved, value paid") or the "Worth it." close.

## Launch order
Ep 1 → 2 → 3, then season order. The first three plant the lens: the flip
(coffee), value lives in people (diamond), price ≠ value (sale). Everything
after is built on those three.

## Connecting each short to its chapter (per-post formula)
The video never carries the CTA. The connection lives in the post text,
per platform:
- **TikTok / Reels** (captions aren't clickable): the caption names the
  chapter in words — "From Value, Created — Chapter 6: Every Value Starts
  as a Problem Solved." — then "Want the full 15 minutes? Link in bio."
  The bio link goes to the audiobook show page (all chapters, one tap away).
- **YouTube Shorts** (descriptions are clickable): the **first line** of the
  description is the direct chapter link
  (`https://www.thejoydigi.com/podcasts/value-created/<N>`), then the
  standard caption. Also attach the full chapter as the Short's related
  video. YouTube is the conversion platform — zero friction here.
- Every episode's `package.md` ships both: the chapter line in the caption
  and the YouTube first-line link.
