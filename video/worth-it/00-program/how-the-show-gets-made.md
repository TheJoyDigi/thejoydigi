# How the show gets made

*Worth It — "See the value. Be the value."*
*The short-video arm of the Value, Created audiobook.*

Three lanes. One pipeline. Nothing crosses lanes.

---

## The three lanes

### Long — Executive · the green light
- Approves each script (~5 min)
- Approves each final cut (~10 min)
- Device-tests on his phone
- Posts to @thejoydigi

### Lola — Series Director · the content brain
- Story, season map, scripts
- Claims with receipts (`claims.md`)
- High-level art & voice direction
- QA sign-off on every cut
- Reads the numbers, weekly report to Long

**Boundary (Long's call, 2026-10-02): Lola does not build visuals or video.**
No video samples, no production commits, no tech detail on how video gets
made. Direction is high-level only — **what**, not **how**. Lola's craft is
writing and directing; the agent's is producing.

### Claude agent — Production · the hands
- Builds **all** animation and visual content from the director's scripts
  per `visual-style.md`
- Renders 1080×1920, ~-16 LUFS
- Packages captions, covers, alt text
- Keeps the engine healthy
- Owns every production commit under `01-episodes/` and `02-engine/`

---

## The pipeline — one episode

| # | Stage | Owner | Done when |
|---|-------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | **Pick** | Lola | Episode chosen from the season map, ordered by hook strength |
| 2 | **Script** | Lola | Myth → reframe → flip → one move; `claims.md` receipts filed |
| 3 | **Hook** | Lola | First 2 seconds land the myth on frame 0; cover frame chosen |
| 4 | **Build** | Agent | Scenes per `visual-style.md`; Lens, Counter, Myth Strike, Move Card |
| 5 | **QA gate** | Agent + Lola | Automated checks pass **and** Lola ticks mute, freeze-frame, philosophy |
| 6 | **Render + voice** | Agent | Silent + voiced MP4s (voice direction: Lola; the mix: agent) |
| 7 | **Package** | Agent | Caption with audiobook CTA, pinned question, alt text, cover |

**◇ GATE — LONG.** Watches the final cut (~10 min). Nothing posts without
his eye on it.

| # | Stage | Owner | Done when |
|---|-------|-------|-----------|
| 8 | **Launch** | Long | Real-device test, then post to @thejoydigi (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) |
| 9 | **Learn** | Lola | Numbers at 48h and 7d; decisions logged; loops back to Pick |

---

## The capture chain — why the show exists

**Short (30s) → Audiobook (15 min) → Book → Direct relationship.**

The CTA ("want the full 15 minutes? link in bio") lives in the caption and
profile bio — never burned into the video.

---

## Non-negotiables

- Works on mute.
- The myth is attacked, never the viewer.
- Every number has a receipt.
- No hype, no shame, no financial-product pitches.
- Calm, adult, confident — warm editorial, never cartoon.
