# Growth plan — Worth It

Brand: **The Joy Digi** umbrella; credit line "Worth It is a production of
The Joy Digi". Voice: warm, clear, confident, no hype. Handles and profile
text still to decide.

**How to read this plan.** A research pass (2026-10-01) found that **no
platform publishes effect sizes** for hooks, pacing, loops, or posting times,
and most "rules" online are folk wisdom. This plan separates what platforms
state, what we guess, and what we measure. Each guess becomes a test.

## What the platforms actually say
- **Instagram:** top signals are watch time, likes, sends. It will not
  recommend content that explicitly asks for shares, comments, or tags;
  open-ended questions are fine. Reels insights show a skip rate for the
  first 3 seconds.
- **YouTube Shorts:** "viewed vs swiped away" is the retention measure.
  From 2026-08-24 a view counts at the first frame; "engaged views" exclude
  loops, so raw views are inflated. Never judge by raw views.
- **TikTok:** likes, shares, comments, follows, finishing longer videos.
- **What we will not trust:** "hook in 3 seconds" as a rule, any published
  "ideal retention" percentage, "loops count double", a universal best
  posting time.

## Hook shapes for this show (hypotheses)
- **The myth, stated flatly:** "The five-dollar coffee is keeping you broke."
  (Then the strike.) Contradicting common advice is the fastest pattern
  interrupt we have.
- **The impossible object:** "In a desert, water beats diamonds." Curiosity
  before context.
- **The quiet number:** "Skill is the only input that compounds." A sentence
  that sounds wrong until it's obvious.
- Under 8 words on screen. The myth is on frame 0.

## The reframe test
Our version of the hook lab: the first six episodes alternate **seeing**
episodes (1, 2, 3) with **capture** episodes (8, 9, 5). If capture outperforms
seeing on completion and saves, the audience wants the harder medicine and we
lean in. This is weak evidence (topics differ) but it's our own audience's.

## Caption and comments
```
[the reframe in plain words, e.g. "Problem solved, value paid."]
Move: [the one action]
Worth It  Ep N/12  |  A production of The Joy Digi
Educational only, not financial advice.
```
- **Pinned comment, Receipts:** sources for any number used. Trust is the product.
- **Pinned comment, an open question:** e.g. "What's something you paid for
  this week that was worth it?" Never "comment X" or "share this".
- Reply to good questions with a video reply; it becomes bonus content.

## Cadence
3 posts a week, ~4 weeks per season. Post order by hook strength, not chapter
order: **Ep 1, Ep 5, Ep 3** lead.

## What we measure
| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Skip / swiped-away rate | does the myth hold |
| Completion and average % viewed | does the arc hold |
| Saves | does the move feel useful |
| Sends per reach | the signal Instagram says matters most |
| Comments restating the reframe | did they *see* it ("I never thought of it like that") |
| Follows per movement | is the series building an audience |

Set our own baseline from the first six posts. Look at 48 hours and 7 days.
Log every decision in `season-map.md`.

## Not doing
- Chasing trends that contradict the content rules.
- Clickbait claims we can't back up.
- Engagement bait, on screen or in captions.
- Sponsors, affiliate links, product picks.
- Hype aesthetics. Our confidence is clarity.
