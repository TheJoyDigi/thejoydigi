02 / The compression

Keep the insight. Leave room for depth.

Roughly 2,000 words become 85. The reduction works because each short carries one idea—not a miniature chapter.

chapter words

Director’s content pass

  1. Extract the one idea in one sentence.
  2. Name the myth that idea kills.
  3. Stage the reframe as one drawable picture.
  4. Flip the viewer’s starting question.
  5. Give one concrete move.
Myth → Reframe → Flip → Move → “Worth it.”

04 / The promise

The short resolves a thought—not the whole subject.

That boundary makes both formats stronger. The short is useful on its own; the long form rewards the curiosity it creates.

The short delivers

  • One myth worth challenging
  • One image the viewer can remember
  • One shift in how to see the problem
  • One move they can make next

The audiobook holds

  • The stories behind the idea
  • The evidence that makes it credible
  • The nuance that prevents overreach
  • The chapter’s place in the full system
