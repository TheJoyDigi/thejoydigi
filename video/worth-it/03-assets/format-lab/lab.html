Style mix

Voice

Bed

A pad chord per story beat: tense, bright, open, resolved. Quiet under the voice.

Effects

Always

  • One idea per beat, about 8 words max
  • Frame 0 shows the hook; it becomes the cover
  • Switch visual mode every 3 to 5 seconds
  • Show it, then say it
  • Works on mute

Never

  • Money-hype: no $$$, rockets, luxury flexing
  • Strike or shame Wren or the viewer
  • Red on anything but the myth
  • Hold one mode so long it goes flat
  • Kid-targeting cues in titles or covers
