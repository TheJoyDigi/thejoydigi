The message

Some nights the take is off. The sunrise does not check how many tries it took. You press record anyway.

How it is animated

The figure holds each pose and only its hair and scarf move, on threes (8 drawings per second). Rain runs at 12. The background drifts smoothly. Speed lines and one inverted impact frame carry the drama, the way limited animation does.
