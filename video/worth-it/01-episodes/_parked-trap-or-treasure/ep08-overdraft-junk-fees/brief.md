# EP08 (Trap 8 / 24): The $2 You Were Short

**World:** 2, Vault Valley (saving)  |  **Length target:** 30 s  |  **Status:** idea (brief only, nothing verified for screen yet)

## Hook (on screen, frame 1, max 8 words)
You were $2 short. It cost $35.

## The trap (named)
Overdraft and "junk" fees turn a tiny shortfall into a big charge.

## The one idea (one sentence)
A small gap can cost far more than the gap. You can see it coming and often switch it off.

## Visual metaphor (one only)
A moth eating holes in your wallet, one bite per mistake.

## The Snare
Fee Moth. Stands for the mechanism, never a company.

## X-ray: who profits
Follow the money: a $2 shortfall, the fee arrow, the moth. Compare the gap with the fee.

## Treasure: the way out
Set a low-balance alert, keep a small buffer, and ask your bank what overdraft coverage you have and how to turn it off.

## Evidence (see `../../00-program/evidence.md`)
- REPORTED: 15% of banked ages 18 to 29 paid an overdraft fee in the prior year (Federal Reserve SHED, 2025).
- VERIFIED (qualitative): BNPL users are more likely to overdraw their checking account (FINRA Foundation NFCS 2024, p. 25).
- CHECK before scripting: the typical fee amount today, and whether the CFPB overdraft rule is in force. The $35 in the hook is a placeholder until sourced.

## Quest (one tiny free action)
Turn on a low-balance alert, and find out whether overdraft coverage is on.

Tense (trap), bouncy (X-ray), bright (treasure).
