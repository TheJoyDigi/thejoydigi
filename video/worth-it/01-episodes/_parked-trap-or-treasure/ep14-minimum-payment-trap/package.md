## Pinned comment 1: Receipts
```
Receipts:
- Interest is added to what you owe each month, and paying only the minimum can take years: CFPB Building Blocks, "Understanding minimum payments", Summer 2022. https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_building_block_activities_understanding-minimum-payments_guide.pdf
- US card statements must show how long minimum-only payoff takes and the total cost: Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.7(b)(12). https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/7/
- Example only: $1,000 at 24% APR, minimum = interest + 1% (floor $25), no new purchases. Minimum only: 74 months, about $887 interest. A fixed $50 every month: 26 months, about $290. The minimum itself shrinks as you pay ($30 down to $25), which is why it takes so long. Real cards vary. A common minimum formula: https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/how-is-your-credit-card-minimum-payment-calculated
```
