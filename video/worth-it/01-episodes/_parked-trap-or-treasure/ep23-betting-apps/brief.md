# EP23 (Trap 23 / 24): The Free Bet

**World:** 6, Fortress (protect and recover)  |  **Length target:** 30 s  |  **Status:** idea (brief only, nothing verified for screen yet)

## Hook (on screen, frame 1, max 8 words)
"Free $50 bet." What is the catch?

## The trap (named)
Betting apps use bonuses, near-misses and speed to make losing feel like almost winning.

## The one idea (one sentence)
The house edge is built into every bet. A bonus is a hook, and the catch is in the wagering rules.

## Visual metaphor (one only)
A slot-machine-shaped trap with the lever hidden inside your phone.

## The Snare
Jackpot Jester. Stands for the mechanism, never a company.

## X-ray: who profits
Follow the money: show the odds math with a simple example (to compute and verify before scripting), who keeps the edge, and how a "free" bonus is paid back.

## Treasure: the way out
Set limits before you start, treat any betting as paying for entertainment, and know where to get help.

## Evidence (see `../../00-program/evidence.md`)
- VERIFIED: 31% of adults under 30 bet on sports in the past year (36% of men, 29% of women); 17% bet online (Pew Research Center, July to August 2025, n = 9,916).
- REPORTED: legalization lowered household net investing by about 14% (NBER working paper w33108, all ages).

## Quest (one tiny free action)
