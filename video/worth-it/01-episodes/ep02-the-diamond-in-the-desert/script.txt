They say you get what you pay for.

Not in a desert.

In a desert, a glass of water beats a diamond. Same diamond, same water, the value moved. It was never in the thing. It's in the person.

So stop asking what it's worth.

Ask the better question. Who is it worth it to?

A thing is only valuable if it solves someone's problem.

Before your next purchase, ask: whose problem does this solve, mine or the ad's?

Problem solved, value paid.

Worth it.
