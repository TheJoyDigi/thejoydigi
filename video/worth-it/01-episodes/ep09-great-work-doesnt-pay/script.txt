They say do great work and you'll be rewarded.

Tell that to the chef.

He makes the meal. The owner keeps the margin. Same food, same night. One is rich. It isn't the cook. Creating isn't enough. Someone always captures. Rarely the maker.

So stop asking how to work better.

Ask the better question. Who captures the value you create?

Find the leak. Then plug one.

Name one way to keep, say, ten percent more of what you make.

Problem solved, value paid.

Worth it.
