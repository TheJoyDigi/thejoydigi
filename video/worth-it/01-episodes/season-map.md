# Worth It — Season 1 map (12 episodes)

**Premise:** the Value, Created thesis in 30-second shorts. One everyday transaction
per episode. Every episode runs the same arc:

```
THE MYTH    the common advice everyone repeats      (pattern interrupt)
THE REFRAME where the value actually was            (see it)
THE FLIP    the better question                     (the turn)
THE MOVE    one action, under 5 minutes               (do it)
SIGN-OFF    "Worth it."
```

The season itself has an arc: **Seeing → Creating → Capturing → Scaling → Giving.**
It starts with learning to see value and lands on giving it away — which is the
book's final move and Long's own play (Nextdoor, The Joy Digi).

**Leads alternate by episode number:** odd episodes (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11) star **Wren**; even episodes (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12) star **Kai**. Same arc, same devices; only who lives the moment changes. See `../00-program/visual-style.md`.

## PART I — SEEING VALUE

### Ep 1 — The Five-Dollar Coffee (Ch 6: Every Value Starts as a Problem Solved) ✅ voiced
- **Myth:** "The latte is keeping you broke."
- **Reframe:** Someone solved a problem — a warm cup, a place to sit, ten quiet minutes — and you gladly paid five dollars for it. Problem solved, value paid. That's how money moves.
- **Flip:** Stop asking "how do I spend less?" Ask: what can I make that someone would pay five dollars for?
- **Move:** Write down one thing people already ask you for help with.

### Ep 2 — The Diamond in the Desert (Ch 1: What "Value" Actually Means)
- **Myth:** "Expensive things are valuable things."
- **Reframe:** The diamond-water paradox: in a desert, water beats diamonds. Value was never in the thing — it's in the person holding it.
- **Flip:** Don't ask what it's worth. Ask *who* it's worth it to.
- **Move:** Before your next purchase, ask: whose problem does this solve — mine, or the ad's?

### Ep 3 — The Sale Price Is Lying (Ch 4: Price Is Not Value)
- **Myth:** "A discount means you're winning."
- **Reframe:** Price is a rumor about value. The "was two hundred dollars" was never real — the rumor moved, the value didn't.
- **Flip:** Ignore the rumor. What's it actually worth to you?
- **Move:** Price one thing you own by what it does for you, not what you paid.

## PART II — CREATING VALUE

### Ep 4 — The Slow Way to Get Rich (Ch 7: The Inputs — Time, Effort, Skill)
- **Myth:** "Just work harder."
- **Reframe:** Time and effort are spent; they're gone. Skill is the only input that compounds — it grows while you sleep.
- **Flip:** Which of your inputs grows without you?
- **Move:** Give this week's extra hour to the skill, not the shift.

### Ep 5 — The Most Boring Way to Get Rich (Ch 13: Removing Friction)
- **Myth:** "You need a brilliant idea."
- **Reframe:** Every wait, click, and hassle erased is value created. The quiet fortunes are shorter lines and fewer forms.
- **Flip:** What do the people around you complain about?
- **Move:** Erase one tiny friction for one person this week.

### Ep 6 — The Invisible Dividend (Ch 12: Trust, the Invisible Ingredient)
- **Myth:** "Trust is soft. Numbers are hard."
- **Reframe:** Trust is the most valuable line item that never appears on a balance sheet — it cuts the cost of every deal, every hire, every sale.
- **Flip:** Who trusts you — and what does that save them?
- **Move:** Keep one small promise today, out loud.

### Ep 7 — Stop Trying to Be Original (Ch 14: Recombination)
- **Myth:** "Innovation means inventing something new."
- **Reframe:** Innovation is mostly rearrangement. Camera plus phone. Sushi plus burrito. Two old things, one new value.
- **Flip:** What two things do you know that nobody's combined?
- **Move:** Write down one mashup. Most will be bad. You need one.

---

## PART III — CAPTURING VALUE

### Ep 8 — A Raise Is a Price (Ch 16: Pricing — Naming Your Worth)
- **Myth:** "Be grateful. Don't ask."
- **Reframe:** A raise isn't a reward, it's a price — and pricing is naming what the value is worth. Cost-plus begs; value-based names.
- **Flip:** What value did you create this year — in one number?
- **Move:** Write the number down before any negotiation.

### Ep 9 — Great Work Doesn't Pay (Ch 15: Creating Isn't Enough)
- **Myth:** "Do great work and you'll be rewarded."
- **Reframe:** The people who make things go broke — the capture problem. The chef makes the meal; the owner keeps the margin.
- **Flip:** Who captures the value you create?
- **Move:** Name one way to keep ten percent more of what you already make.

### Ep 10 — The Salary Is Not the Prize (Ch 20: Ownership)
- **Myth:** "Land the good salary and you're set."
- **Reframe:** Owners keep what workers create. Equity is the capture machine — a small piece of something beats all of nothing.
- **Flip:** What's the smallest piece of ownership you can start?
- **Move:** Look up what one share of anything costs. Start there.

---

## PART IV — EXCHANGING & SCALING

### Ep 11 — The Most Expensive Choice (Ch 21: Trade — The First Leverage)
- **Myth:** "Self-made means doing it all yourself."
- **Reframe:** Trade is the first leverage. Do one thing well, trade for the rest — the specialist plus trade beats the self-sufficient every time.
- **Flip:** What's your one thing — and what will you trade for?
- **Move:** Trade away one task this week instead of doing it.

---

## PART V — MASTERY

### Ep 12 — Give It Away (Ch 28: Give Value First)
- **Myth:** "Protect what you know."
- **Reframe:** Generosity as strategy. Delivered value becomes reputation becomes opportunity — givers end up with the most. (This is the season's thesis and the show's own business model.)
- **Flip:** What can you give that costs you nothing?
- **Move:** Give one useful thing away today. No strings.

## Production notes
- **Length:** ~30s each, Voice-first; video can follow.
- **Post order:** by hook strength, not chapter order (same rule as the existing plan) — lead with Ep 1, Ep 5, Ep 3.
- **Each episode ends:** the move + "Worth it." — the verdict doubles as the brand.
- **Season 2** pulls from the remaining chapters (use vs. exchange, needs/wants/pain, knowledge as raw material, service by hand, attention, scarcity and moats, systems, brand, money frozen, markets, leverage, distribution, networks, compounding, reputation flywheel, choosing what to build, your system, teach it).
