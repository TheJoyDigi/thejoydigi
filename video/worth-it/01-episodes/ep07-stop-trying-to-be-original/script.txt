They say be original. Invent something the world has never seen.

Nobody does.

The camera phone wasn't invented. A camera and a phone were combined. Sushi burrito. Two old things, one new value. Innovation is mostly rearrangement.

So stop staring at a blank page.

Ask the better question. What two things do you know that nobody's combined?

You already hold the pieces.

Write down one mashup today. Most will be bad. You only need one.

That's the game. Problem solved, value paid.

Worth it.
