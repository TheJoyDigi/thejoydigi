Everyone loves a good sale. Fifty percent off!

But the sale price is lying to you.

That was two hundred dollars was never real. The price is just a rumor about value, and rumors move. The jacket didn't change. Only the rumor did.

So ignore the sticker.

Ask the better question. What's it actually worth to you?

Not what they say it was. What it does for you.

Take one thing you own. Price it by what it does, not what you paid.

That's the game. Problem solved, value paid.

Worth it.
