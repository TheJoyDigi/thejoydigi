# EP19 (Trap 19 / 24): The Screenshot Is Not Proof

**World:** 5, Seed Garden (investing)  |  **Length target:** 30 s  |  **Status:** idea (brief only, nothing verified for screen yet)

## Hook (on screen, frame 1, max 8 words)
He turned $500 into $50,000. (screenshot)

## The trap (named)
"Get rich" posts and screenshots sell hope. The person showing the win often earns from your follow or your sign-up.

## The one idea (one sentence)
Do not judge money advice by how rich it looks. Ask who pays them, what the downside is, and whether anyone can check.

## Visual metaphor (one only)
A fishing lure: the shiny part is not the point.

## The Snare
Hype Hawk. Stands for the mechanism, never a company.

## X-ray: who profits
Follow the money: the hawk shows only winning screenshots; the arrow runs from your follow and sign-up to the hawk. Survivorship: the losers are not posted.

## Treasure: the way out
The Receipts test: who pays them, what is the downside, can I verify it. We hold ourselves to it and publish our sources.

## Evidence (see `../../00-program/evidence.md`)
- REPORTED: 52% of Gen Z investors trust sources that share their own financial performance; 37% cite an influencer as a factor in starting; 50% made a FOMO-driven investment (FINRA Foundation, Gen Z and Investing, 2022).
- REPORTED: SEC charged 8 influencers in a $100M pump-and-dump (Dec 2022).
- VERIFIED context: the show itself is a money voice, so this episode must hold us to the same test.

## Quest (one tiny free action)
Pick one money influencer you follow and answer the three Receipts questions.
