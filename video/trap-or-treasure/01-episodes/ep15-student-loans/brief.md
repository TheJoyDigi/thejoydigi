# EP15 (Trap 15 / 24): What You Sign For

**World:** 4, Interest Swamp (debt)  |  **Length target:** 30 s  |  **Status:** idea (brief only, nothing verified for screen yet)

## Hook (on screen, frame 1, max 8 words)
35% of 18 to 34 year olds have one.

## The trap (named)
Student loans are signed in a hurry and repaid for years, and many borrowers never learn the rate, the plan or the date.

## The one idea (one sentence)
Know three things about your loan: the balance, the rate, and the monthly payment. Late payments cost more than the payment.

## Visual metaphor (one only)
A long road with a toll booth you did not see.

## The Snare
Fine-Print Fox. Stands for the mechanism, never a company.

## X-ray: who profits
Follow the money: how interest adds up over time on a sample loan (to compute and verify before scripting).

## Treasure: the way out
Find your three numbers, set autopay, and know your repayment options.

## Evidence (see `../../00-program/evidence.md`)
- VERIFIED: 35% of ages 18 to 34 have student loans (FINRA Foundation NFCS 2024, p. 23).
- VERIFIED: among student-loan holders with payments due, 42% were late at least once in the past year, and 54% worry they cannot pay them off (same page).
- CHECK: interest accrual while in school, current repayment plans and rates. These change. Source the federal student aid site, no private lenders.

## Quest (one tiny free action)
Log in to your loan account or the federal student aid site and write down balance, rate and monthly payment.

Tense (trap), bouncy (X-ray), bright (treasure).
