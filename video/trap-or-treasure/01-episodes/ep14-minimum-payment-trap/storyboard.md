## Pacing check
Each beat has one new thing. One style per beat, four in total (kinetic type with a close-up, anime with phone UI, Pearl print with flat graphics, game UI). The biggest element on screen is always the number or the face.

## Audio plan (generated by code, rendered offline)
128 bpm. Kick every beat. Tense pulse (trap), bouncy claps and plucks (X-ray), four on the floor with bright stabs (treasure), chord on the quest card. Sound effects (clicks, clinks, stamp thuds, the Snare's laugh, gulp and poof) on every on-screen action. No dependence on sound for meaning.
