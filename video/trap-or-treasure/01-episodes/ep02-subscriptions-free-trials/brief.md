# EP02 (Trap 2 / 24): Free Trials That Never End

**World:** 1, Starter Village (money basics)  |  **Length target:** 30 s  |  **Status:** idea (brief only, nothing verified for screen yet)

## Hook (on screen, frame 1, max 8 words)
Free for 7 days. Then $12.99. Every month.

## The trap (named)
A free trial that auto-renews, plus forgotten subscriptions, turns attention into a quiet monthly bill.

## The one idea (one sentence)
A free trial is a bill with a delay. Small recurring charges add up to real money, and they only stop if you stop them.

## Visual metaphor (one only)
A leech on a calendar: one small sip every month.

## The Snare
Renewal Leech. Stands for the mechanism, never a company.

## X-ray: who profits
Follow the money: the card, a monthly drip, the leech filling up. A counter shows a year of drips (arithmetic: $12.99 x 12 = $155.88, illustrative).

## Treasure: the way out
Put the cancel date in your calendar the day a trial starts. Once a month, scan your recurring charges.

## Evidence (see `../../00-program/evidence.md`)
- REPORTED: 57% of ages 18 to 25 had unwanted subscription charges (YouGov for Bankrate, Jan 2022, n = 2,497, private survey).
- REPORTED: FTC secured a $2.5B settlement with a large retailer over sign-up and cancellation tricks (Sept 2025).
- CHECK: whether the FTC "click to cancel" rule is in force today.

## Quest (one tiny free action)
List every recurring charge in your bank app and cancel one.
