# EP22 (Trap 22 / 24): Your Own Paycheck, Three Days Early

**World:** 6, Fortress (protect and recover)  |  **Length target:** 30 s  |  **Status:** idea (brief only, nothing verified for screen yet)

## Hook (on screen, frame 1, max 8 words)
$5 to get your own money 3 days early.

## The trap (named)
Cash-advance apps and payday loans look cheap per use but cost a lot as a yearly rate, and they can become a habit.

## The one idea (one sentence)
Turn any fee into a yearly rate: fee divided by amount, times 365 divided by days. Small fees on short loans are big rates.

## Visual metaphor (one only)
A coin sack with a turnstile: every pass costs a coin.

## The Snare
Payday Piranha. Stands for the mechanism, never a company.

## X-ray: who profits
Follow the money: $100 for 14 days with a $5 fee is about 130% a year (arithmetic: 5/100 x 365/14, illustrative). Show the formula.

## Treasure: the way out
Run the fee through the formula before you tap. Build a tiny buffer so you do not need the advance.

## Evidence (see `../../00-program/evidence.md`)
- VERIFIED: 46% of ages 18 to 34 used at least one non-bank borrowing method (payday, pawn, rent-to-own, auto title, tax refund advance) in the past five years; 15% of all adults used a payday loan (FINRA Foundation NFCS 2024, p. 24).
- REPORTED: a CFPB data spotlight gives an illustrative 109.5% APR for earned-wage advances with 27 draws a year.
- CHECK: current rules and fee structures. Do not name any app.

## Quest (one tiny free action)
Take any fee you have paid and turn it into a yearly rate.
