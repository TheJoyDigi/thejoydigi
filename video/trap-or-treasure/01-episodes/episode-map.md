## Launch order is by hook strength, not curriculum
The worlds are a map. The post order is a hook decision. Lead with the traps that reach the most people and are easiest to feel: **16 Pay in 4**, **2 Free Trials**, **14 Minimum Payment**, **21 Scam Radar**, then the rest. Within a world, post the strongest hook first. See `../00-program/growth-plan.md` for the calendar tied to real decision moments.
