# Growth plan

Brand: **The Joy Digi** umbrella; credit line "Trap or Treasure is a production of The Joy Digi". Voice: warm, clear, a little playful, no hype. The TikTok handle and profile text are still to decide (README decision 7).

**How to read this plan.** A research pass (`2026-10-01`) found that **no platform publishes effect sizes** for hooks, pacing, loops or posting times, and that most "rules" online are folk wisdom. So this plan separates what platforms state, what we guess, and what we will measure. Each guess becomes a test.

## What the platforms actually say
- **Instagram:** top ranking signals are watch time, likes and sends. It will not recommend content that **explicitly asks** for shares, comments or tags; open-ended questions are fine. Reels insights show a skip rate for the first 3 seconds. Source: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/ and https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/718151/
- **YouTube Shorts:** "viewed vs swiped away" is the retention measure. Recommendations are described as independent of length. From 2026-08-24 a view counts at the first frame, and "engaged views" exclude loops, so raw views are inflated. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281
- **TikTok:** likes, shares, comments, follows, and finishing longer videos. No "3-second rule" is published. Source: https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you
- **What we will not trust:** "hook in 3 seconds" as a rule, any published "ideal retention" percentage, "loops count double", "cuts every 0.5 seconds", a universal best posting time.

## Hook rules (our hypotheses, tested by the Hook Lab)
- The hook is on screen and moving on **frame 1**. No logo intros.
- Three kinds (`series-bible.md`): **A** Mei's face with the number, **B** the Snare in silhouette, **C** text only.
- Shapes that fit this show: **number shock** ("You paid $30. Debt dropped $10."), **question** ("Where did my $20 go?"), **mistake** ("The free bet has a catch"), **promise** ("Cancel it in 2 minutes").
- Under 8 words on screen. Keep the first two seconds inside the safe box.

## The Hook Lab test
Rotate A, B and C across the first nine episodes, three each, spread across worlds so topic does not decide the result. Compare each episode's skip rate and completion with the account baseline. This is weak evidence (topics differ) but it is our own audience's evidence. After the first nine, drop the loser and test a new variant against the winner.

## Caption and comments (see `_template/package.md`)
```
[the hook in plain words, plus the trap's plain name for search, e.g. "minimum payment trap"]
Quest: [the tiny action]
Trap or Treasure  Trap #N  World N  Ep X/24  |  A production of The Joy Digi
Educational only, not financial advice.
```
- **Pinned comment 1, Receipts:** the sources. Trust is the product; this is how we earn it.
- **Pinned comment 2, an open question:** "Which trap got you?" Never "comment X", "share this" or "reply done".
- Hashtags are a small set that names the topic. They are a starting guess; review every two weeks.
- Reply to good questions with a video reply; it becomes bonus content.

## Cadence
- **3 posts a week** for about 8 weeks, produced a world at a time. A Buffer study of 11.4M TikToks (within-account, Oct 2025) found 2 to 5 a week gave the biggest gain per post. It is correlational and skews to small business accounts.
- Bonus shorts fill gaps and test hooks cheaply.
- **Post order is by hook strength, not curriculum order.** Lead with the broadest, easiest-to-feel traps: **16 Pay in 4**, **2 Free Trials**, **14 Minimum Payment**, **21 Scam Radar**.

## Content calendar: just-in-time

## Platform features
Use YouTube Shorts series, Instagram "Watch Part 2" and TikTok Playlists. None has a published growth effect; they fit the numbered format. Repeat the Trap File card on every episode so the collection grows.

## Repurpose
- The same MP4 goes to Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Upload natively to each platform.
- The 720p share copy goes to Slack, Discord and email.
- The cover frame is chosen in `package.md`; frame 0 already carries the hook.

## What we measure
| Metric | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Skip / swiped-away rate** | IG skip rate, YouTube "viewed vs swiped away" | does the hook hold |
| **Completion and average percent viewed** | all three | does the structure hold (loops can push above 100%) |
| **Sends per reach** | IG insights | the signal Instagram says matters most |
| **Saves** | all three | does the Treasure card feel useful |
| **Comments that name a trap** | all three | are people placing themselves in the story |
| **Followers per world** | all three | is the series building an audience |
| **Rewatch of Nickel episodes** | where available | do series devices work |

- **No benchmarks exist.** Set our own baseline from the first six posts and compare every later post with it.
- Look at 48 hours and at 7 days. Write each decision in `episode-map.md`.
- Never judge by raw views on YouTube Shorts (first-frame counting).

## Not doing
- Chasing trends that contradict the content rules.
- Clickbait claims we cannot back up.
- Engagement bait on screen or in captions.
- Engagement bait about money amounts people may feel bad about.
- Sponsors, affiliate links or product picks.
