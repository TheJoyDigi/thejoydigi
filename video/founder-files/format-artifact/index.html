01
The formula · every episode, no exceptions

One claim that sounds wrong. Three beats that prove it.

Every Founder Files episode runs the same four blocks. The template is fixed by the show bible; Ep01 below is the real cut, timed to the script's word count, with every hard cut and every in-frame build marked.

Series templateShow bible
Ep01 · AirbnbProjected VO timing
Cut rhythmEp01
Hard cut (beat change)In-frame build: a number, name or line lands

0 to 2s

Hook

One sentence with a specific number, high stakes and a curiosity gap. A claim that sounds wrong until the story proves it.

  • Frame 0 is complete. Big kinetic type of the hook line, readable with sound off.
  • Never "Did you know", never a question the viewer can answer "no" to.

About 25s · 3 beats

Story

Setup (the hole they were in), Move (the unlikely thing they did), Payoff (the number).

  • Numbers are the stars: every number and name is kinetic type.
  • Hard cuts between beats. Nothing holds still longer than about 2s.
  • Historical beats get real archival texture, never stock.

About 8s

Lesson

One Value, Created principle, named with its chapter. The story is the principle wearing a disguise.

  • The only place the tempo drops. One takeaway card, words land on the VO.

About 4s

End card

"The full breakdown is in Value, Created, Chapter N." Book cover plus thejoydigi.com.

  • Same card every episode (series identity). Only the chapter number changes.
Timing check

The Ep01 script is 117 spoken words including the CTA. At a brisk, dry read of about 2.7 words a second (a production assumption until the VO is recorded) it lands at 43.3s: inside the 30 to 45s window with no script cuts. The script's slot targets are rebalanced to the words: Beat 3 carries 34 words, which need about 12.6s rather than the 27 to 34s slot (8s). The CTA read (12 words, about 4.4s) runs slightly past the bible's 3.8s end card, so it either reads a touch faster or starts on the lesson's tail. All cuts lock to the recorded VO. If Lola would rather trim Beat 3 than run that pace, that is a script call, not a production one.

Style frames · Ep01 · gold luxe

Frame A · 1080 × 1920
AHook

"$40" over the box

0:00.0 to 0:02.2 · VO: "Airbnb was saved by breakfast cereal."

The claim sounds wrong, and the price sounds wrong for cereal. Two curiosity gaps in one frame, readable before the first word lands.

Motion notes
Frame 0
Complete composition, no fade-up. The hook line is fully set on frame 0: it is the sound-off first read, the feed thumbnail, and the loop point when the reel replays.
Enter
"$40" slams from 120% scale to 100% in 6 frames, ease-out cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), with a 1.5% undershoot that settles over 3 more frames so it lands with weight. Full opacity from frame 0: no blur, no shake. The cereal box silhouette rises from 16% below over 15 frames behind the type, gold rim light on its leading edge.
Build
"A box · 2008" tracks in at f8, letter-spacing closing from 0.9em to 0.42em over 10 frames.
Hold
Whole frame pushes 100 to 104% across the shot. Nothing sits dead still.
Exit
Hard cut on the end of "cereal" straight into "2008" (Beat 1). No transition, no whoosh.
Frame B · 1080 × 1920
BBeat 2 · The move

Obama O's & Cap'n McCain's

0:08.1 to 0:17.8 · four builds, each landing on a stressed word

The unlikely move, shown as the object itself: two cheap boxes, hand-glued, side by side. Blue left, red right, gold price underneath.

Motion notes
Enter
Cut in on "So the founders". "1,000" counts up from 0 on tabular lining figures (width never jitters) and lands exactly on "thousand" at +1.9s. "boxes of cheap cereal." wipes on left to right behind it in 10 frames.
Build
Obama O's box drops in on "Obama" (+4.8s): falls 14% from above, overshoots its -4° tilt, settles in 12 frames like it was set down on a table. Cap'n McCain's mirrors it on "Cap'n" (+5.9s) at +3°.
Hold
Both boxes sway ±0.6° on a slow 3s sine, out of phase. Paper grain, glue edges and tape stay visible: it should feel made by hand at a kitchen table, not rendered.
Payoff
"$40 a box" slams on "forty" (+8.1s), same 6-frame slam as the hook so the number rhymes. "500 of each design" tracks in under it.
Exit
Hard cut on "box" into "$30,000".

Archival rule: the final cut uses real photographs of the 2008 boxes (show bible: real artifacts, not stock). These boxes are a stand-in drawn in SVG to lock layout, scale and colour; sourcing and rights for the photos are confirmed before picture lock.

Eight moves. Nothing else.

A small, strict vocabulary is what makes a series feel expensive. Every episode is built from these eight behaviours, timed in frames at 30fps. Sound sits under it the same way: one dry, confident narrator and a subtle bed, no whooshes and no hype stingers.

06
What this page asks Long to approve

Approve the look, then the pilot gets made.

  1. Palette & typeNear-black, champagne gold, white Noto Serif Display. Archival colour only inside real artifacts.
  2. The hook frameHook line complete on frame 0, giant kinetic number, object silhouette behind it.
  3. The motion vocabularySlam, count, wipe, rise, hairline, hard cut, push, tempo. Nothing else.
  4. Cut rhythmHard cuts on beat changes, a build at least every 2 to 3 seconds, the lesson as the only slow frame.
  5. The end cardLocked as the series constant for every episode.
  6. Two open itemsThe $20,000 source (Lola) and the Beat 3 pacing call (keep at about 2.7 words a second, or trim).
