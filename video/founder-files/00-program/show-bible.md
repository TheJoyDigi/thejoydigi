# Founder Files — Show Bible
**Working title:** Founder Files
**Status:** Director package v1 — Long approved the series 2026-10-08. Claude owns all production.

## The show in one line
Real founder stories, 40 seconds each: how a real company won (or almost died), ending in one Value, Created principle.

## Format
- 30–45 seconds, vertical 9:16, built for Reels / TikTok / Shorts.
- Cadence: 2–3 per week (Tue / Thu / Sat). Daily would dilute it; weekly is too sparse to build a habit.
- Long posts every episode himself after device-testing. Nothing publishes without his sign-off.

## Episode structure (every episode, no exceptions)
1. **HOOK (0–2s):** One sentence. Specific number + high stakes + curiosity gap.
   Good: "Airbnb was saved by breakfast cereal." / "She built a billion-dollar company with a pair of scissors." / "This is just water. It's worth $1.4 billion."
   Bad: "Let me tell you about Airbnb." / "Success leaves clues."
2. **STORY (3 beats, ~25s):** Setup (the hole they were in) → Move (the unlikely thing they did) → Payoff (the number).
3. **LESSON (~8s):** One sharp principle the story proves, stated lightly. This is the brand's voice, not a pitch: state the principle, never name the book or a chapter. ("The cereal wasn't the business. Resourcefulness was. Price is never about the thing. It's about the story." Full stop.)
   The story comes first, always. A real, interesting founder story with a light principle beats a forced book lesson every time. If the connection to the book's ideas is natural, keep it. If it feels shoehorned, cut it. Nobody ever shared a video because of its lesson; they share it because the story is great.
4. **END CARD (~3s):** Series mark only. No book cover, no link, no CTA. The account grows on the strength of the stories; the book gets marketed when the audience exists.

## Why no book pitch (Long's call 2026-10-09)
Every episode ending in a pitch turns a great story into an ad, and ads don't get shared. Founder Files is pure content: true founder stories people watch and enjoy. Growth first (followers), book marketing later, when there's an audience to market to. The book can live in the profile and captions; it never interrupts the video.

## Ongoing series, growth-first (Long's call 2026-10-09)
Founder Files is an ongoing series whose job is growing the account. Cadence: 2–3 per week (Tue/Thu/Sat), indefinitely. Pipeline: Lola researches and writes each script (fact-checked, headline numbers verified before shipping), Claude produces the video, Long posts.
- Backlog: `02-backlog/` holds verified draft outlines (Ep04–Ep08 done 2026-10-09: WhatsApp, Mailchimp, Gymshark, Bumble, Patagonia). Keep at least 5 episodes ahead of production.
- Growth mechanics: every episode ends with a soft follow prompt on the end card ("Follow for the next one") — a series CTA, not a book pitch. The series mark + consistent cadence + shareable true stories are the growth engine.
- Threads companion per episode (see below) doubles each script's mileage.

## Threads companion (Long's call 2026-10-09)
Every episode also ships as a Threads-native text version. Not a cross-post: the story retold for a reading audience.
- Post 1: the hook, verbatim ("Airbnb was saved by breakfast cereal.")
- Posts 2-4: the three beats, one post each, tightened for text.
- Final post: the principle, stated plainly. No book mention, no CTA.
- Same rules as the video: no em dashes, every number verified, human voice.
- Lola writes the thread from the episode script; it posts the same day the video goes live.
- The thread lives in the episode's script file under a "Threads version" section.

## Hook rules
- First frame must work with sound OFF (big kinetic type of the hook line).
- Never start with "Did you know" or a question the viewer can answer "no" to and swipe away.
- Every hook makes a claim that sounds wrong until the story proves it.

## Visual + motion direction (WHAT, not how — Claude owns execution)
**Style call (Long, 2026-10-09): warm editorial, modern and young. NOT the book's gold luxe style, NOT neon.** This series has its own visual identity, built for a young audience and for comfortable watching.
- **Palette (researched 2026-10-09, refined by Long):** warm paper base with visible grain and texture. Ink family, not a single accent: ink black as the primary voice, plus vermilion (~#E8491D), indigo (~#2E5BFF), and ochre as supporting inks. One ink per moment, never all at once. Rationale: brighter images outperform dark ones ~24% on IG; warm tones read human and trustworthy for founder stories; neon full-bleed fatigues over 40s and reads "crypto bro."
- **Hand-drawn layer (Long's call):** doodles, shapes, patterns, texture. Hand-drawn arrows, circles, underlines, scribbles, and small sketches that annotate the story like margin notes: circle the $40, arrow to the cereal box, star the payoff number. Paper grain, ink bleeds, rough edges. The feel is a brilliantly illustrated zine or editorial spread, playful but premium, never corporate motion graphics.
- **Look:** bold, contemporary, high-energy. Big punchy type, high contrast, confident and playful. Think the feeds young people actually watch, not a finance book cover.
- **Motion on every beat:** nothing static longer than ~2 seconds. Kinetic type for every number and name (numbers are the stars). Meme-aware pacing: fast cuts, satisfying builds, zero dead air.
- **Archival texture:** historical beats get real artifacts (cereal box, patent sketch, can design), treated in the modern style, not sepia nostalgia.
- **Sound:** dry, confident single narrator with edge. Subtle bed underneath, no cheesy whooshes.
- **End card (3s):** series mark + "Follow for the next one." Same card every episode (series identity).

## Mobile safe zone (Long's call 2026-10-09)
Every frame must be designed for the 9:16 Reels overlay. Keep all key type, numbers, and doodles inside the safe area:
- **Right edge:** keep clear ~170px (1080-wide canvas) for the like/comment/share/music buttons.
- **Bottom:** keep clear ~350px for the caption, profile row, audio title, and progress bar.
- **Top:** keep clear ~200px for the status bar and Reels header.
- **Safe area ≈ x 0–910, y 200–1570** on a 1080×1920 canvas. Nothing important outside it, ever.
- The format artifact shows platform UI zones as a toggleable overlay; every style frame is reviewed with it on. Long device-tests on his phone before anything ships.

## Sourcing rule (non-negotiable)
Real companies, public facts, every number verified before the script ships. Each script lists its sources. Getting a number wrong about a real company torches credibility, and these will get shared. No composite characters, no "a founder I know."

## What makes an episode work
The viewer should finish thinking two things: "I didn't know that" and "I want the next one." If an episode only delivers one, it gets rewritten. The story being real and interesting is the whole game; the principle is seasoning, not the meal.
