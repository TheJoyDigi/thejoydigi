# Founder Files Ep03 — "Water Worth $1.4 Billion"
**Company:** Liquid Death (Mike Cessario) · **Chapter:** Ch19 — Brand: Trust, Stored · **Runtime target:** ~40s (~90 words)
**Sources:** Wikipedia (founded Dec 18 2018; $1.4B valuation March 2024); Inc./Fast Company interviews with Mike Cessario; Sherwood News.

## Script
**HOOK (0–2s):**
This is just water. It's worth one point four billion dollars.

**BEAT 1 — the hole (3–12s):**
2019. Mike Cessario is an ad guy with no beverage experience, no distribution, no money. His product: water. The most boring product on Earth.

**BEAT 2 — the move (13–26s):**
So he puts it in a tallboy can with a skull on it and calls it Liquid Death. Tagline: "Murder Your Thirst." Drinking water becomes a punk rock choice instead of a health chore.

**BEAT 3 — the payoff (27–34s):**
By 2024: a one-point-four billion dollar valuation, nearly eight million followers, in over a hundred thousand stores.

**LESSON (35–40s):**
The product didn't change. The meaning did. That's what a brand really is: trust, stored. Chapter 19 of Value, Created.

**CTA (end card):**
The full breakdown is in Value, Created, Chapter 19. Link in bio.
