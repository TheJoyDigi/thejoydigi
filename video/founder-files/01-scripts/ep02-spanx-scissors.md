# Founder Files Ep02 — "The $5,000 Pair of Scissors"
**Company:** Spanx (Sara Blakely) · **Chapter:** Ch6 — Every Value Starts as a Problem Solved · **Runtime target:** ~40s (~95 words)
**Sources:** businesstraveller.com profile; Forbes (youngest self-made female billionaire 2012); Fortune MPW interview.

## Script
**HOOK (0–2s):**
She built a billion-dollar company with a pair of scissors.

**BEAT 1 — the hole (3–12s):**
1998. Sara Blakely is selling fax machines door to door. Getting ready for a party, she hates the lines under her white pants, so she cuts the feet off her pantyhose.

**BEAT 2 — the move (13–26s):**
No fashion background. No investors. She takes five thousand dollars in savings, writes her own patent from a textbook, and cold-calls hosiery mills until one says yes.

**BEAT 3 — the payoff (27–34s):**
She demos it herself at Neiman Marcus. They put it in seven stores. Oprah names it a Favorite Thing. In 2012, she becomes the youngest self-made female billionaire.

**LESSON (35–40s):**
Every value starts as a problem solved. She didn't find a market. She was the market. That's Chapter 6 of Value, Created.

**CTA (end card):**
The full breakdown is in Value, Created, Chapter 6. Link in bio.
