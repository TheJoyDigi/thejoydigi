# Founder Files Ep01 — "The $40 Box of Cereal That Saved Airbnb"
**Company:** Airbnb · **Chapter:** Ch4 — Price Is Not Value · **Runtime target:** ~40s (~95 words)
**Sources:** Airbnb company timeline; Yahoo Finance via newspointapp.com; Benzinga via tradingview.com; Brian Chesky 2019 interview.

## Script
**HOOK (0–2s):**
Airbnb was saved by breakfast cereal.

**BEAT 1 — the hole (3–12s):**
2008. Airbnb is dying. Almost no users, no investors, twenty thousand dollars in credit card debt.

**BEAT 2 — the move (13–26s):**
So the founders buy a thousand boxes of cheap cereal, repackage them as "Obama O's" and "Cap'n McCain's," and sell them for forty dollars a box.

**BEAT 3 — the payoff (27–34s):**
They make thirty thousand dollars. It keeps the company alive for months. And when Paul Graham sees the boxes, he says: "You guys are like cockroaches. You just won't die." Y Combinator funds them.

**LESSON (35–40s):**
The cereal wasn't the business. Resourcefulness was. Price is never about the thing. It's about the story.

**END CARD:**
Series mark only. No pitch.
