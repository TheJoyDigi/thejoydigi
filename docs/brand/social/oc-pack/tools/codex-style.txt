"Poolside Sticker" style for The OC Pack, a cheerful Orange County, California pet podcast.
- Bold flat vector illustration, die-cut sticker look. Clean, confident, simple shapes that read at small sizes.
- Every shape has a uniform warm-brown outline (#6B3410), never black. Medium-thick line, rounded joins.
- Flat colour fills only, at most one flat shade per shape. No gradients, no textures, no glow, no 3D, no photorealism, no drop-blur.
- Stickers sit on a thick cream (#FFF4DA) die-cut border with a hard offset brown (#6B3410) shadow down-right (no blur).
- Palette ONLY: Sunset orange #FC7F46, Coral #FF6F61, Gold #FEBB55, Sand #FDE3A7, Cream #FFF4DA, Ocean teal #2E9CA6, Lagoon #7CC8C8, Palm green #1F6B5C, Brown ink #6B3410. Pink-coral, never red. No purple, no grey, no pure black, no pure white.
- Mascots: a happy golden retriever (gold #FEBB55 fur, open smile, pink tongue, palm-green collar with a round gold tag) and a smiling orange tabby cat (sunset orange #FC7F46, darker stripes, green eyes, teal collar with a small gold bell). Friendly, simple, round, big dot eyes.
- SoCal poolside motifs: sunburst sun, palm fronds, wavy water lines, paw prints, beach. Use sparingly.
- Lettering, when asked for: chunky rounded heavy display letters (like Titan One), cream fill with brown outline and a hard brown offset shadow. Spell every word exactly as given. Add NO other text, letters, numbers, signage or watermarks anywhere.
