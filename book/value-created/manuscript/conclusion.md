# The Book in One Sentence

The last chapter named six moves: see it, create it, capture it, multiply it, give it first and teach it. Fold them into the book's five parts and you get the whole climb, one line per step, small enough to carry in your head.

1. **See it.** Value is not in things. It is in people.
2. **Create it.** The creator's job is not to invent. It is to notice.
3. **Capture it.** Creation is abundant. Capture is scarce.
4. **Scale it.** Leverage is doing more with less.
5. **Master it.** Teaching is the final leverage.

And if even five lines is too many, the whole book folds down into a single sentence:

> Find a real problem. Solve it well. Charge less than the pain it caused.

Do that, and you have created value. Do it again, for more people, with better tools, and you have a life's work.

The first chapter ended with a small instruction. It still holds, and now you know exactly what it means.

Go find someone who is thirsty.
